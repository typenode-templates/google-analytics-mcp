# Use GSC and GA4 together

Install and connect both sibling MCP servers. Their credentials may be shared if properly configured; their data populations and tools remain separate. These recipes ask the AI client to orchestrate retrieval, not a backend join.

## From search visibility to landing-page activity

> Retrieve GSC search performance and GA4 Google organic landing-page traffic for [site/property choices] and [dates]. Inspect the GA4 property timezone and web streams. Propose an explicit URL alignment rule and show unmatched pages and ambiguous mappings. Keep GSC clicks separate from GA4 sessions and users; don't assert one-to-one reconciliation.

## From landing-page traffic to recorded outcomes

> For aligned pages, retrieve GSC visibility/click evidence and separate GA4 traffic and selected event evidence. Explain the recorded observations and their denominators. Don't claim a particular search query caused an individual's signup: these aggregate tools cannot establish that attribution.

## Compare changes with uncertainty

> Retrieve comparable periods in each server, keeping source, timezone, date lengths, filters and populations explicit. Calculate differences in the client where the measurements support it. Separate supported observations from hypotheses about tracking, intent or causation.

Missing data may reflect source limits, dates, filters, tracking or permissions. A current configuration record is not proof of historical instrumentation. Business pipeline, CRM revenue, qualified leads and unrecorded events require other sources.
